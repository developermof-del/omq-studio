The fonts are unmodified and are supplied for editable surrounding text.
Wordmark v3.0: the OMQ Arabic logotype reconstructs the chosen illustrated
typographic concept as vector contours. It is not set with the IBM Plex
fonts. No font is needed to display it. See logos/OMQ_Logo_Specification.json.
The bundled IBM Plex typefaces serve the surrounding editorial text and interface.
